## 2d6 matrix
2, 12 = 1
3, 11 = 2
4, 10 = 3
5, 9 = 4
6, 8 = 5
7 = 6

## p(sum of 2d6 is n)
Sample space = sides ^ rolls, eg 2d6 = 6^2 = 36.
Find n in 2d6 matrix, divide by sample space.
e.g. where n is 8, p(5/36).

## p(at least one x in n k-sided rolls)
Find the complement.
Eg for p(at least one six in 3d6):
p' = 5/6^3 = 125/216, thus p = 91/216.
